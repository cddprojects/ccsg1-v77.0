2. Opportunity listings

An item marked “Open Opportunity” is intended to represent a confirmed active vacancy. An item marked “Opportunity Area” is an interest category, not a confirmed job opening. Creating a profile or expressing interest is not an application that Flexikits reviews or approves.

Flexikits stores those interests as structured records (listing ID and/or category ID) so a company that later reviews profiles can understand why you appear in a talent view.
