Words people use for flexibility

Place, hours, and workload mean different things. Pick the one you need.

Workplaces in Singapore often talk about flexibility in three ways: where you work, when you work, and how much you take on. The short definitions below follow publicly available workplace guidance. Flexikits did not write that guidance, is not affiliated with the Ministry of Manpower, and cannot say what any employer will offer.

Four steps

How Flexikits works

Look around

Read the interest areas. You do not need to give any details to browse.

Flag what fits

Pick the areas that match your experience, and the flexibility you would like to ask for.

Fill in your details

Your name, email, a number you can be reached on, and the skills that apply to you.

A company may contact you later

If a company reviews profiles in those areas, it may contact you directly. Many people will not hear from anyone. That is expected.

Your profile

One profile for the areas you choose.

Fill in your details once. Add only the areas that genuinely fit you. If a company later reviews profiles, it can see the areas you picked. That is not an application, and it is not a job offer.

Specific areas, not a general CV drop

A reviewer sees the area you picked, not a claim that you applied for an open role.

Yours to change

Ask us to add, correct, or remove your details when your plans change.
