The idea

Start from what you want, not from what is advertised.

Looking for work usually means fitting yourself around whatever is advertised. Here, you say what you are looking for first, and that stays on your profile if a company later reviews it.

If a company is interested, it may contact you directly using the details you gave. Listing your profile is free, and you can change or remove your interests at any time. Flexikits is not an employer and does not place people into jobs.

Flexibility stated up front

Note whether you want flexibility on place, hours, or workload. That is your preference, not a promise that an employer offers it.

No one screening you first

We do not score or rank you. If a company reads profiles, it makes its own call.

Honest labels

If something is not a confirmed vacancy, it is labelled an opportunity area, never a job that is open.

Know what to ask for

Flexibility means three different things. Ask for the one you need.

Workplaces in Singapore often describe flexibility as place, hours, or workload. Knowing which one you need helps you say precisely what you are asking for. Flexikits is not affiliated with the Ministry of Manpower and cannot say what any employer will offer.
