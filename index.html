The idea

Start from what you want, not from what is advertised.

Job hunting usually means fitting yourself around whatever is advertised. Here, you say what you are looking for first, and that stays on your profile for participating companies to review.

If a company is interested, it contacts you directly using the details you gave. Listing your profile is free, and you can change or remove your interests at any time.

Flexibility stated up front

Note whether you need flexibility on place, hours, or workload, so it is clear from the start.

No one screening you first

We do not score or rank you. Companies read profiles themselves and make their own call.

Honest labels

If something is not a confirmed vacancy, it is labelled an opportunity area, never a job that is open.

Know what to ask for

Flexibility means three different things. Ask for the one you need.

The Ministry of Manpower groups flexible work into three official categories. Knowing which one you need helps you say precisely what you are asking for.
