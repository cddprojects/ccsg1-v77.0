Digital support areas

Digital support areas you can register interest in.

These areas cover customer support, admin, content review, e-commerce support, and AI data quality work. They are types of work you can register interest in, including if you need part-time or other flexible hours or load. Confirmed vacancies, if any, are marked Open Opportunity.

Know what to ask for

Flexible work is not only working from home.

Flexible work can mean part-time or a lighter load (Flexi-Load), different hours with the same total (Flexi-Time), or a different place (Flexi-Place). Since 1 December 2024, employees in Singapore can make a formal Flexible Work Arrangement request. The employer should give a written decision within two months. Flexikits does not send that request for you. Use the three terms below so a company can see what you actually need. Current guidance is on the Ministry of Manpower website.
