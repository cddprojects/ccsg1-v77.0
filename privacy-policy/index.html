Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

1. Who we are

Flexikits (“we”, “us”) is a Singapore-focused website that lets individuals explore opportunity areas, express interest, and create a profile. Flexikits is not the employer for opportunity areas unless a listing expressly says otherwise, is not an employment agency, and does not decide which candidates a company contacts.

2. Personal data we collect

When you create a profile, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • WhatsApp / mobile number, including country code
  • Work area interests, including listing IDs and category IDs you selected
  • Skills you select from the list provided
  • Optional marketing preference, if you opt in separately

We do not collect NRIC or other national identity numbers at this stage.

3. Purpose of collection

We collect this information so that:

  • your profile can record the opportunities or areas you expressed interest in;
  • a company that later reviews profiles can understand why a candidate appears in their talent view;
  • that company may contact you directly using the contact details you provide;
  • we can operate the profile, communicate about the account or profile itself, and meet legal obligations.

Opportunity-related contact and unrelated promotional marketing are separate purposes. Marketing messages are sent only if you opt in to that optional control.

4. Disclosure to companies

By creating a Flexikits profile, you acknowledge that the information you provide may be made available to companies that later review profiles for the purpose of considering your interest in relevant areas. A company may contact you directly using the contact information you provide. Creating a profile does not mean a company is reviewing profiles today.

This disclosure is also shown next to the submit control on the profile form. It is not intended to be hidden only inside this policy.

5. What we do not do with this data

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use opportunity-contact consent as consent for unrelated marketing.
  • We do not decide, on a company’s behalf, that you are suitable for a role.

6. Retention, security, and your rights

We keep your profile only for as long as it is needed to record your interest, or until you ask us to remove it.

You may ask us to access your data, correct it, or withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent means your profile stops being available for later review.
